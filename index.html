PHASE: Compliance-Enabled Tactile Phase Retrieval for Few-Shot Insertion Learning
Jeremy Siburian, , , , ,
International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) 2026
Paper Website
A compliant wrist lets robots sustain contact throughout insertion, producing tactile and force signals that reveal the phases of a task. Retrieving prior experience phase by phase enables few-shot insertion learning, improving success rates by 13 percentage points over the strongest non-phase-aware baseline.

Online Robot Manipulation Error Detection and Description
Yung-Shun Chan, Kuniaki Saito, , Jeremy Siburian, , Chun-Yi Lee
Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL) 2026
Website
Robot manipulation failures can be detected as the video streams, predicting when each failure begins and ends along with a natural-language cause-and-effect description. Pre-training on temporally grounded annotations generated automatically from unlabeled DROID trajectories yields a 73% relative gain in failure localization.

Tactile Memory with Soft Robot: Robust Object Insertion via Masked Encoding and Soft Wrist
Tatsuya Kamijo*, *, Nodoka Shibasaki, Jeremy Siburian, ,
Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) 2026
Paper
A soft wrist allows safe contact exploration during data collection, while tactile memory reuses past demonstrations through retrieval. Masked encoding of tactile, force-torque, and proprioceptive signals enables robust insertion that adapts to unseen pegs and conditions, without explicit subtask segmentation.
